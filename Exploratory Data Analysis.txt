Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) & Business Intelligence
1. Descriptive Statistics
Calculated key statistical measures for numerical variables:
* Mean
* Median
* Standard Deviation
* Minimum
* Maximum
Analyzed:
* Price
* Number of Subscribers
* Number of Reviews
* Content Duration

2. Univariate Analysis
Created visualizations to understand data distribution:
* Price Histogram
* Subscriber Histogram
* Review Distribution Analysis
Key findings:
* Most courses are priced in lower ranges.
* Subscriber distribution is highly skewed.
* A small number of courses attract very high engagement.




3. Business Questions Analysis
Answered key business questions using Pivot Tables and Excel analysis tools.
Questions addressed:
1. Which subject has the highest number of subscribers?
2. Which courses have the highest review counts?
3. Which course level has the highest enrollments?
4. What is the average course price by subject?
5. What is the distribution of paid versus free courses?

4. Multivariate Analysis & Correlation
Performed relationship analysis among numerical variables using:
* Scatter Plots
* Correlation Matrix
* Heat Map
Variables analyzed:
* Price
* Number of Subscribers
* Number of Reviews
* Content Duration
Key finding:
* Reviews and subscribers show a positive correlation, indicating that highly reviewed courses tend to attract more enrollments.





5. Dashboard Development
Created an interactive dashboard summarizing the most important insights.
Dashboard Components:
* Total Courses KPI
* Total Subscribers KPI
* Average Price KPI
* Total Reviews KPI
* Price Distribution
* Subscriber Distribution
* Subject Analysis
* Paid vs Free Course Analysis
* Correlation Heatmap
* Key Business Insights

Tools Used
* Microsoft Excel
* Pivot Tables
* Pivot Charts
* Histograms
* Scatter Plots
* Conditional Formatting
* Dashboard Design

Key Insights
* Most Udemy courses are priced at lower price ranges.
* Subscriber counts are highly concentrated among a small number of courses.
* Beginner and All-Level courses attract significant enrollments.
* Paid courses dominate the platform.
* Reviews have a strong positive relationship with subscriber growth.
* Course duration has a moderate relationship with pricing.
